```text
STYLE: BIB Editorial (from icoone Booklet 2026)

INTENT
- Luxury beauty-magazine feel, not a sales brochure. One idea per page.
- Image leads, words follow. Large white space. Calm, soft, high-key.
- Reader feels first (image + one line), then reads proof (data, cases) later in the sequence.

FORMAT
- Portrait page (A4 ratio ~1:1.41). Generous margins (~8% of width). Left-aligned text.
- Mix full-bleed image pages with clean white information pages. Never two dense pages in a row.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Display (Latin): very thin geometric sans (ExtraLight/Light), ALL CAPS, huge (≈15–20% of page width per letter line),
  2–3 stacked lines, set bottom-right, allowed to bleed off the right edge. Example: "BODY / AESTHETIC".
- Thai headings: light weight, large, 2 short lines, no bold.
- Thai body: regular, short paragraphs; bold only the key phrase inside a sentence.
- Small labels: spaced caps, small size (case labels, expert names).
- English emotional line in lowercase, light weight, with Thai translation under it between em dashes.

COLOR
- Base: white / warm off-white. Text: graphite/charcoal grey.
- One accent colour per chapter (flat, muted pastel):
  Body = blush #E1C3B8 · Face = sand #DBD189 · Medical/Recovery = mist #D4DDDC.
- Gold-taupe (~#B8A086) for expert names and small accents. Slate (~#4A5560) for table label cells.
- No gradients, no glow, no neon.

IMAGERY
- Close crop of the body part the page talks about (abdomen, face, back, legs). Soft natural light, matte skin,
  light background. Faces calm, eyes closed or soft gaze.
- Real device photos only, shown in use by a therapist's hands, or as a clean cut-out on white.
- Expert portraits: small circular, black-and-white.
- Before/after: same angle, on a measurement grid backdrop, paired side by side.
- Interlude pages: monochrome soft-grey body silhouette, full bleed, text centered on top.

PAGE TYPES (reuse these)
1. COVER: full-bleed close-up photo; brand logo top-left in white; 2-line light tagline bottom-left in white.
2. CHAPTER OPENER (photo): full-bleed body-part photo + giant thin caps title bottom-right + 4 small hashtags stacked top corner.
3. CHAPTER DIVIDER (colour): solid chapter colour, same giant thin caps title bottom, hashtags top-right in white. Nothing else.
4. STORY/TECH PAGE: Thai light heading (2 lines) → short body with bolded key phrases → image band with a grey text panel
   ("3 technologies in one") → quote block with circular B&W expert photo and name in gold spaced caps.
5. VOICE + CASES PAGE: top photo strip with 4 customer-voice lines in quotation marks (bold Thai);
   a thin chapter-colour band; then 3 case rows: [label + aim + plan bullets] [pill: gender icon, age, program icons]
   [before/after pair] with a caption bar in chapter colour naming the source clinic.
6. EMOTIONAL INTERLUDE: grey silhouette, centered 2 short Thai stanzas (first line bold, second light),
   English lowercase line + Thai translation.
7. LIST PAGE: short Thai lines separated by thin hairline rules; product photo fills the lower half.
8. FAQ: big "FAQ" title; Q in light, A in bold; very large faint letters (Q, &, A) in the background.
9. PRESS/PROOF: collage of real covers/logos on the left; right column of thin Thai headlines separated by rules.
10. GALLERY GRID: 2×3 alternating logo tiles and photos on light grey.
11. BACK COVER/CTA: black-and-white space photos on top; centered invitation line; logo; contact; QR in a taupe badge.

SEQUENCE (rhythm of the whole piece)
Cover → product hero → what it is (story/tech) → 4 chapter openers → programme overview → emotional interlude →
for each chapter: colour divider → voices + cases → feature list → FAQ → press → gallery → back-cover CTA.

COPY VOICE
- Headline = feeling or human truth; sub-line = the practical benefit.
- Use real customer voices in quotes to open a chapter (pain in their words).
- Numbers appear one at a time, large, with source in small text.
- Close every piece with an invitation to try and see for yourself.

AVOID
- Symmetric card grids on every page, dashboards, dense tables on story pages, icons-as-decoration, emoji,
  floating fabric/candles/flowers/stones as props, stock smiles, gradients.
```
